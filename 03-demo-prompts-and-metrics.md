# Demo Prompts & Success Metrics

## Demo scenario

A customer support team wants to reduce repetitive questions while keeping responses accurate, helpful, and on-brand.

## Example prompts

### Support agent prompt

You are a customer support assistant for a SaaS company. Answer the customer using the available knowledge base. Keep the response clear, friendly, and concise. If the question cannot be answered confidently, escalate to a human agent.

### Sales enablement prompt

Summarize the key customer pain points, relevant product features, and suggested next steps for a sales follow-up email. Keep the tone consultative and practical.

### Internal knowledge prompt

Find the most relevant internal guidance for this question and summarize it in plain language. Include assumptions, risks, and where the user should go for more detail.

## What I would measure

- Activation rate  
- Repeat usage  
- Resolution rate  
- Escalation rate  
- Customer satisfaction  
- Time saved  
- Quality of generated responses  
- Trust and confidence from users  

## Product marketing angle

The demo should not just show that the agent works. It should show why the agent matters, what problem it solves, and how the customer can imagine using it in their own workflow.
