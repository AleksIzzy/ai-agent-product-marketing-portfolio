# Customer Use Cases for AI Agents

## 1. Customer Support Agent

Helps answer common customer questions, route complex issues, summarize context, and support human agents with suggested responses.

**Customer value:** faster support, better consistency, less repetitive work.

## 2. Sales Enablement Agent

Helps sales teams find product information, prepare for customer calls, summarize account context, and generate follow-up materials.

**Customer value:** faster preparation, more relevant conversations, better use of internal knowledge.

## 3. Internal Knowledge Agent

Helps employees find answers across documentation, policies, product materials, and internal resources.

**Customer value:** less time searching, fewer repeated questions, faster onboarding.

## 4. Content Workflow Agent

Helps teams repurpose content, create first drafts, adapt messaging for different audiences, and maintain consistency.

**Customer value:** faster content production, clearer messaging, more scalable creative workflows.
