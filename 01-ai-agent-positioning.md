# AI Agent Positioning

## Simple positioning

AI agents help teams automate repetitive workflows, answer questions, take action, and support customers or employees using natural language.

The strongest positioning is not “AI that can do everything.” It is “AI that helps a specific team complete a specific workflow faster, more accurately, and with less manual effort.”

## Target customer

Operations, customer support, sales, enablement, and internal knowledge teams that deal with repeated questions, fragmented information, and manual workflows.

## Core message

AI agents help teams move from manual, repetitive work to guided, automated workflows that are easier to scale.

## Example value pillars

- Faster response times  
- Better access to internal knowledge  
- Reduced repetitive work  
- More consistent customer or employee experience  
- Scalable support without losing quality  

## Messaging principle

Do not sell the model. Sell the outcome.
